Third-party data on this site
=============================

The rain map on the Weather tabs uses data from three sources. Each keeps its own licence;
the licence of this site's code does not apply to them (docs/adr/0008-map-and-weather-data-licences.md).


1. Circuit outlines: bacinger/f1-circuits (MIT)
-----------------------------------------------

Source: https://github.com/bacinger/f1-circuits, commit 8094e957b8d6aa8b9f4510c93968556cccd30e02.
Changes: each outline is projected to km around the circuit's centre, simplified (15 m),
clipped to 6 km and rounded.

From the bacinger/f1-circuits README: "This repository is unofficial and is not associated in any
way with the Formula 1 companies." "Formula 1™ circuits data are not official, and they are not
approved nor endorsed by Formula One Licensing B.V."

2. Water, coast and roads: OpenStreetMap (ODbL 1.0)
---------------------------------------------------

Contains information from OpenStreetMap, which is made available here under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ — © OpenStreetMap contributors, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

The layers are a Derivative Database under the ODbL 1.0, extracted (one Overpass query per
circuit), projected, simplified and clipped by scripts/map-extract.ts --osm, and kept as
separate files (apps/web/src/map/osm/). Anyone may copy them under the ODbL 1.0.


3. Rain: Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0)
-----------------------------------

Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (https://open-meteo.com/), under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: model precipitation is sampled at 7 x 7 points around the circuit, each 15-minute
sum is shown as mm/h (x 4), and the values are binned into three steps (light, moderate,
heavy). It is modelled, not radar.

For Silverstone the model data includes UK Met Office data, under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
